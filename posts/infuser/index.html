INFUSER: Influence-Guided Self-Evolution Improves Reasoning

Tuesday, Jun 9, 2026 | 1 minute read | Updated at Tuesday, Jun 9, 2026

@ Siyu Chen, Miao Lu, Beining Wu, Heejune Sheen, Fengzhuo Zhang, Shuangning Li, Zhiyuan Li, Jose Blanchet, Tianhao Wang, and Zhuoran Yang

AUTHORS: Siyu Chen, Miao Lu, Beining Wu, Heejune Sheen, Fengzhuo Zhang, Shuangning Li, Zhiyuan Li, Jose Blanchet, Tianhao Wang, and Zhuoran Yang

LINKS: arXiv: Paper  |  GitHub: Code

INFUSER turns self-evolution into influence-guided curriculum learning. A co-evolving generator drafts questions and reference answers from unstructured documents, while a solver trains on them with correctness rewards.
